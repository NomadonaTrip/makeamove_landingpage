House rules

The house lights are up. Every rule here fixes something the apps got wrong.

  • Three matches a day

    Not a thousand faces. A few people picked for you, so each one gets your full attention.

  • Deal-breakers first

    Genotype, faith and state of origin come up before the first date, not after the third. Built for Nigerians and Africans at home and abroad.

  • Nobody gets ghosted

    Walk away whenever you like. You just tell them why, in your own words.

  • Manners count

    Showing up and answering on time raise your standing. Disappearing lowers it. Only you ever see your own penalties.

  • Real, single and over 18

    Every contestant verifies a government ID with a live selfie. Married people can't join, and anyone can report someone who slips through.

  • An alarm on every date

    One button shares your exact location if a meeting ever feels wrong.
